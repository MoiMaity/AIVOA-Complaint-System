Subject: Product Quality Complaint - Black particulate matter in Metformin HCl IP, Batch MET-2605-118

Dear Quality Assurance Team,

We are writing to formally raise a product quality complaint against material
supplied under the batch detailed below.

Customer Name: Meridian Pharma Ltd, Slough, UK
Product Name: Metformin Hydrochloride IP
Product Strength: 99.2% assay, IP/BP grade
Batch No: MET-2605-118
Mfg Date: 12/05/2026
Expiry Date: 11/05/2029
Quantity Affected: 45 kg
Complaint Date: 14/07/2026

During dispensing for our FDF granulation line on 11 July 2026, our production
operators observed black to dark grey particulate matter distributed through the
powder in two of the twenty five kilogram HDPE drums received against the above
batch. The particles were visible against the white powder bed and ranged in
size from approximately 0.5 mm to 2 mm. Dispensing was halted immediately and
both drums were quarantined under hold label QH-2026-0442.

Our in-house microscopy indicated the particles are non-metallic and appear
fibrous in nature, possibly gasket or filter media. No metal detector alarm was
triggered. The remaining drums from the consignment have been placed on hold
pending your response.

No material from this batch has been released into finished product, and no
patient exposure has occurred. However, given the nature of the contamination we
consider this a serious quality defect and request a formal investigation report
within 30 calendar days in line with our quality agreement.

Retained samples of the affected drums are available for your inspection.
Photographs are attached separately.

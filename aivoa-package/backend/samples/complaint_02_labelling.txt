Description of issue:

Nordvik's warehouse team identified during goods-in inspection that the outer
carton for the above batch displays the expiry date as 04/2028 while the blister
foil printed within the same cartons shows 03/2028. The discrepancy was found
across an estimated 1200 packs of a 4000 pack shipment. Batch coding on the
blister is otherwise legible and the product itself appears conforming.

No product has been distributed to pharmacies. The affected packs remain in
quarantine at the Oslo distribution centre. Nordvik requests written
confirmation of the correct expiry date and instruction on whether the affected
packs should be relabelled under supervision or returned.

Nordvik notes that a similar carton coding discrepancy was reported against
batch ATV-2512-077 in December 2025 and asks whether the corrective actions from
that investigation were effective.
